The five AI-use levels

The UoC policy names five broad levels. The Npuls AI-waaier describes five practical categories for assignments and assessment. The labels below show both vocabularies to help users discuss the permitted role of AI.

1 · No AIUoC: AI-free / Npuls: Geen AI. The task is completed without AI.
2 · Plan with AINpuls: Plannen met AI. Ideas, planning and structure; submitted work shows the student's own development.
3 · Improve with AINpuls: Verbeteren met AI. Editing, feedback and refinement with critical student review.
4 · Collaborate with AINpuls: Samenwerken met AI. AI contributes to parts of the work; the student evaluates, adapts and takes responsibility.
5 · Innovate with AINpuls: Innoveren met AI. Creative exploration and co-creation, with human direction and accountability.

Important: The UoC policy document also uses the descriptions “AI as sparring partner”, “AI as assistant”, “AI as co-creator” and “fully AI”. These are related descriptions, not an exact one-to-one renaming of the Npuls categories. The instructor must state the actual permitted activities for each assignment.

Responsible-use checklist

  • Follow the assignment-specific instructions and ask the instructor if uncertain.
  • Do not enter personal, sensitive, confidential or organisation-restricted information into public AI tools.
  • Check facts, references, calculations and generated content against reliable sources.
  • Remain responsible for the accuracy, originality and integrity of submitted work.
  • Disclose and reference AI use in submitted work in accordance with UoC guidance and applicable citation rules.
  • Keep human oversight in decisions that affect students, staff or assessment outcomes.

This tool provides guidance and does not determine academic misconduct, grant permission to use AI, or replace UoC policy, faculty rules, the OER or an instructor's assessment authority.
